? FAQ

Questions?
Answered.

How does the crypto escrow work?
+
When a deal is agreed, the brand deposits the agreed amount into secure escrow via Dexpay. Funds are held until the creator delivers and KPIs are verified. Once confirmed, funds release automatically to the creator's wallet. If there's a dispute, our admin team mediates — protecting both parties throughout.
What's the difference between an intro deal and a performance deal?
+
An intro deal pays a flat fee for the affiliate introducing a brand to a creator — a one-time finder's fee. A performance deal pays ongoing commissions based on the traffic, sign-ups, or revenue the affiliate drives. Both are tracked transparently on the platform.
How quickly are payouts processed?
+
Once KPIs are verified, escrow release is automatic. Dexpay transfers typically settle within 1–4 hours depending on network congestion. You choose your payout wallet during onboarding — crypto only for escrow payouts, with fiat conversion support available via our partners.
What percentage does AFF Starter take?
+
A flat 5% transaction fee on completed deals, taken from the escrow release. Agency plans drop to 3.5%. No hidden fees, no monthly charges on the free tier, and no charges if a deal falls through before escrow release.
Is AFF Starter only for iGaming brands?
+
Our core focus is iGaming and crypto brands, which is where our verified creator network is strongest. However, the platform works for any brand that wants to run streamer campaigns or affiliate programs. Our compliance tools are specifically optimized for regulated gambling markets.
How does dispute resolution work?
+
Either party can open a dispute from their dashboard. Our admin team reviews campaign deliverables, KPI evidence, and communication history within 48 hours. The decision is binding — escrow is either released, partially released, or refunded. Less than 2% of deals ever reach formal dispute.
