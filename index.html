What is crypto escrow and why does it matter?+
Crypto escrow means your campaign budget (USDT or USDC) is held in a Dexpay wallet — inaccessible to anyone until the streamer delivers verified content. This eliminates payment disputes and ensures streamers only get paid when KPIs are met. TRC20 and ERC20 networks are both supported.
Can I try before committing to a minimum term?+
The Aff Tester tier has a 2-month minimum and is available once per brand — it's a genuine trial. You get 8 booking requests, 5 affiliate sources, and 3 affiliate intros. Most brands upgrade within their first term.
Which streaming platforms do you cover?+
The streamer directory covers Twitch, Kick, and YouTube. Each profile includes verified channel metrics, content history, and commercial rates. TikTok Live expansion is underway.
What's the difference between sources and intros?+
Affiliate sources are your traffic partner slots — SEO sites, PPC buyers, influencer channels driving casino traffic. Intros are platform-mediated introductions to new affiliate partners. After a successful intro you lock in a revenue-share, CPA, or hybrid deal.
What social add-ons are available?+
Twitter/X management ($1,500/mo), Telegram management ($1,500/mo), X + Telegram bundle ($2,250/mo), full Social Driver ($4,000/mo), and one-time Discord server setup ($1,000). All monthly packages include 30 posts/month and growth strategy.
How are disputes handled?+
Either party can raise a dispute inside the platform. An admin reviews evidence from both sides and issues a resolution — which may include full or partial escrow release, refund, or rescheduling. The process is documented and fully auditable.
